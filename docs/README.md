## SDG Alignment

FoodLink supports:

- **SDG 2: Zero Hunger** by helping recipient organisations discover and collect surplus-food donations.
- **SDG 12: Responsible Consumption and Production** by encouraging redistribution of usable surplus food rather than disposal.
